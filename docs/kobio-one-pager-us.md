# Kobio

**Turn a two-minute voice note into a client-ready quote.**

The fastest path from "I've walked the job" to "here's your number." Built for the contractor who quotes from the truck.

---

## The idea

A contractor walks a job. He already knows the price. He says it out loud.

Kobio listens to that voice note and drafts the quote. Line items, quantities, measurements, prices, the math, the totals. All of it, in the way he actually talks.

The number he speaks always wins. The catalog is memory, not the master. Kobio drafts about 60% of the quote. The human finishes the rest and sends it.

That split is the product. It is not a limitation.

## Why now

Speed is the whole game in home services.

The contractor who answers first wins the job 78% of the time. Reply inside five minutes and you convert nine times better than the one who waits an hour. The industry average is around 42 minutes. Formal estimates still take three to ten days.

At the same time, the work is there and the people are not. The trade is short somewhere between 350,000 and 500,000 workers this year. Roughly 40% of it retires by 2031. The owner's own hours are the scarcest thing in the whole business.

Every quote written at night, after a full day on the tools, is time that cost him twice.

Kobio moves the quote from days to minutes. On site. In his own words.

## Built to be trusted

Two rules the tool never breaks.

Code owns the numbers. The AI owns only the words. Every line is computed exactly as dictated. If two lines do not add up, Kobio flags it. It never quietly corrects a figure. A wrong number reaching a client is the one failure that matters, and the whole design exists to never cause it.

Nothing reaches a client without a human. Every quote is a draft until the contractor says send.

## Different from what is out there

The tools getting attention right now point AI at the customer. They answer the phone. They talk to the homeowner. Contractors are already finding that the robot voice pushes away the higher-paying clients.

Kobio never speaks to the homeowner. It works for the contractor, behind the scenes, on the part of the job he would rather not do. That is a quieter idea. It is also a more defensible one.

## Where it stands

Working product. Live pilot with a real contractor in Europe. It reproduces his actual quotes to the cent.

It was built trade-agnostic from the first line. A painter today, a roofer or an electrician tomorrow, the same engine underneath. And it is built to hand the finished quote straight into the accounting tools contractors already use to invoice.

## How it scales for teams (on the roadmap)

One contractor is where this starts. A crew is where it grows.

On a team, the field records and the office decides. Every estimator speaks their voice note from the job. The drafts all land in one place. The office becomes the validation center. One desk, where every quote is reviewed, corrected, and sent.

The rule does not change. Nothing reaches a client without a human. On a crew, that human sits in the office.

The field moves faster. The office keeps control. The owner sees every number before it leaves the building.

The engine is the same one working today. The validation center is the next piece to build.

## The opportunity

Florida alone is a 60 billion dollar industry. Ninety percent of its contractors are small. Roughly 64,000 small construction employers. A dense, real place to start.
