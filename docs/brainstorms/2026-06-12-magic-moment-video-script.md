# Magic-moment clip — script & shot list

**Goal:** ~30s. Show a messy spoken on-site note become a finished, priced quote.
Lead with the artifact, not the pitch. Builder-to-builder.

**Production model:**
1. **Frank** screen-records the app while speaking the French note below → the
   app produces the quote on camera.
2. **Nicolas** dubs his voiceover (Section C) over the final cut.
The audio Frank speaks is only to *drive the app*; the published audio is
Nicolas's dub.

**Why this note:** it's the validated Amina fixture (`src/quote/fixture.ts`) —
it reproduces **exactly**, TTC **4'715.60** at 8,1 %, from Nicolas's real catalog
rates (pose intissé 65.–/m², code 007). Using it means the "magic" cannot
misfire on camera. **You may change only the client name** (computation is
unaffected). If you change any dimension, rate, or the VAT, re-run it through the
app and confirm the total before filming — don't trust an unvalidated note live.

---

## A. The note Frank speaks (verbatim, to drive the app)

> « Créer une offre pour Madame Rossi, titre : Offre pour dépose de papier peint
> et pose. Arrachage de papier peint et préparation des fonds sur les murs de
> 5,25, 2,90 et 7,60, sur une hauteur de 2,76, à 13 francs du mètre carré.
> Repose du papier peint intissé sur 5,25, 2,90, 2,16, 2,42 et 7,60 sur 2,76, à
> 65 francs le mètre carré. Déplacement, tri des déchets et nettoyage de chantier
> à 150 francs forfait. TVA 8,1 pour cent. »

Speak it like you're standing in the room — a little rushed, naturally. The point
is that it sounds like a real site note, not a script.

## B. Shot list (what to capture)

| # | Time | On screen | Note |
|---|------|-----------|------|
| 1 | 0–3s | Thumb hits the record button; waveform moving | Tight, vertical. No intro card. |
| 2 | 3–10s | Recording while the note is spoken | Let the messy dimensions be audible/visible. |
| 3 | 10–14s | Tap stop → "transcription / extraction" state | The brief wait *is* the tension. |
| 4 | 14–24s | Lines populate: areas computed from the dimensions, rates filled, HT/TVA/TTC ticking up | This is the payoff — linger here. |
| 5 | 24–28s | Finished quote, **TTC 4'715.60** visible; finger scrolls the clean lines | Show it's editable, real. |
| 6 | 28–30s | Cut to Kobio logo + CTA caption | See Section D. |

The hero beat is #4: he *spoke* "5,25, 2,90 et 7,60 sur 2,76" and the app did the
m² arithmetic and priced it. Make sure that's legible.

---

## C. Nicolas's dub-over (casual, builder-to-builder)

Loose and real — not an ad read. Adjust to his own words; this is the intent:

> « Bon, normalement ce devis-là, je le fais le soir, sur Excel, une demi-heure.
> Là je parle… (beat) …et c'est déjà fait. Les m², les prix, la TVA — tout est
> calculé. Moi je relis, je corrige deux trucs, et j'envoie.
> Franchement, essaie. »

Keep it under the visuals — the silence during the "wait" (shot 3) lands harder
than narration over it.

## E. Before you publish

- Confirm the on-screen total reads **4'715.60** (proves you used the known-good
  note).
- If filming for EU groups (FR/BE), remember the quote shows **CHF** today —
  either film a CHF version for CH groups first, or wait for the currency-display
  requirement so the EUR version is honest. See the requirements doc.
